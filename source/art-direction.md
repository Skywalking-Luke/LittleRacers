# Little Racers: Art Direction per Game
Style for all: smooth storybook cartoon (see style-ref.jpg). Rounded vehicles with big friendly eyes on the windscreen, soft gradients, thick soft charcoal outlines, calm pastel palette from theme-guide.md, lots of empty space, gentle idle motion (slow bob, blinking eyes).

## Age 2
1. Beep Beep! | Scene: quiet taman street, morning. Objects: car, bus, motorbike, bomba truck lined up at the kerb. Mood: cheerful. Tapped vehicle bounces and its eyes smile.
2. Car Wash | Scene: shady car wash under a rain tree. Objects: muddy car with soft brown splotches, sponge, soap bubbles, hose. Mood: playful. Mud wipes away under the finger; the car sparkles and grins.
3. Garaj Peekaboo | Scene: row of 3 colourful garage doors in a kampung yard. Objects: lorry, teksi, jeep peeking out. Mood: surprise and giggles. The door rolls up slowly and the vehicle peeks out.

## Age 3
4. Colour Parking | Scene: top-down shopping-mall car park, painted bays. Objects: 4 cars in dusty red, mustard, teal, lavender with matching bays. Mood: tidy and calm.
5. Traffic Light | Scene: simple junction near a school, zebra crossing. Objects: big friendly traffic light with a face, one car. Mood: steady and safe.
6. Build My Car | Scene: cosy workshop with pegboard tools. Objects: body, wheels, roof parts on a tray. Mood: proud. The finished car does a happy honk and a short drive.

## Age 4
7. Count the Cars | Scene: toll plaza at golden hour. Objects: toll booths, a queue of cars, big number buttons. Mood: warm.
8. Road Maze | Scene: top-down town map with houses, school and a pasar malam with lantern stalls. Objects: one car, soft dotted path. Mood: exploring.
9. Shape Garage | Scene: hillside with three tunnels shaped as circle, square and triangle. Objects: cars carrying shape signs on the roof. Mood: puzzle-solving.

## Age 5
10. Letter Road | Scene: winding road through padi fields. Objects: floating letter balloons, a bus or car. Mood: adventurous but calm.
11. Big Little Race | Scene: friendly racetrack with flags and a cheering crowd of trees. Objects: motorbike, car, van, bus, lorry. Mood: excited but soft.
12. Helper Trucks | Scene: small town. Fire: a little smoke from a house window (no flames on people), the bomba arrives. Hurt knee: child sitting on a bench with a plaster, ambulance parked. Breakdown: car on the roadside with hazard lights, tow truck arriving. Mood: brief concern, then relief. Always end on "Semua selamat" with everyone smiling.
13. Balik Kampung Road Trip | Scene: highway to kampung, then a petrol station, durian stall, and Atuk's stilt house. Objects: family car, Atuk waving. Mood: warm and homely.

## Bedtime: Goodnight, Little Jeep
Night palette, moonlight, glowing windows, vehicles with sleepy half-closed eyes, stars appearing. Very slow fades. Nothing sudden.
