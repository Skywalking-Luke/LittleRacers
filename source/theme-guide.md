# Little Racers: Calm Theme Guide
Aim: gentle, low-stimulation play for ages 2 to 5.

## Colours
Soft, muted pastels on a warm off-white background. Avoid neon and pure saturated red.
- Background: cream #FAF6EE
- Sky: soft blue #BFDDEB
- Grass: sage #B9D3A8
- Road: warm grey #C9C3B8, white dashes #FFFFFF
- Cars: dusty red #D98E82, mustard #E8C872, teal #7FB7B0, lavender #B7A9D6
- Text/outlines: soft charcoal #4A4A48 (never pure black)

## Motion
- Slow, eased movement; cars drive at a gentle pace.
- No flashing, strobing, screen shake or fast zooms.
- Rewards are small: a soft sparkle, a happy bounce, a wave. No fireworks or confetti storms.
- One moving thing at a time where possible.

## Sound
- Soft background music, off by default or very quiet, with a mute button.
- Gentle horns and engines, rounded and short. No sirens louder than speech.
- Voice is always the loudest, clearest sound.

## Layout
- Few elements on screen, lots of empty space.
- Big rounded shapes, thick soft outlines.
- No timers, countdowns, scores or streaks.

## Scenes (Malaysian, calm)
- Kampung road with coconut palms and a wooden house.
- Quiet taman (housing estate) street with a playground.
- Toll plaza in soft evening light (Count the Cars).
- Pasar malam with warm lantern glow, no crowds (Road Maze).
- Gentle rain option: soft drizzle, no thunder.

## Session pacing
- Each game is 3 to 5 short rounds, then a calm "All done! Well done!" / "Dah siap! Syabas!" screen.
- Suggest a break after about 10 minutes of play.
