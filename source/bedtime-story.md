# Goodnight, Little Jeep / Selamat Malam, Jip Kecil
Interactive bedtime story and lullaby for ages 2 to 5. Ground transport only, nothing scary. Follows theme-guide.md, but dimmer: dusk to night palette (navy #2E3A59, dusky lilac #8C86B0, warm lamp yellow #F2D894, cream text #FAF6EE). Slow fades between scenes, about 8 to 10 seconds of narration each. One gentle tap per scene, never required: the story moves on by itself.

## Scene 1: Sunset in the kampung
Visual: Little Jeep parks by a wooden house as the sky turns orange. Tap: Jeep blinks its headlights.
- "The sun is going down. Little Jeep is home after a big day." / "Matahari terbenam. Jip Kecil sudah pulang selepas hari yang panjang."

## Scene 2: Bus says goodnight
Visual: Big Blue Bus rolls slowly into the depot. Tap: the bus doors close with a soft "pssh".
- "Big Blue Bus is resting too. Goodnight, Bus!" / "Bas Biru pun berehat. Selamat malam, Bas!"

## Scene 3: Lorry turns off its lights
Visual: a sleepy lorry under a tree. Tap: its lights fade off one by one.
- "Lorry yawns. Lights off, one, two." / "Lori menguap. Lampu padam, satu, dua."

## Scene 4: The last train
Visual: a train glides slowly past paddy fields under the moon. Tap: a soft "choo" and puffs that turn into stars.
- "The night train goes slowly, slowly, past the paddy fields." / "Kereta api malam bergerak perlahan-lahan, melalui sawah padi."

## Scene 5: Motorbike tucks in
Visual: a little motorbike under a blanket-shaped cover. Tap: the cover settles gently.
- "Little Motorbike snuggles under its cover." / "Motosikal Kecil berselimut."

## Scene 6: Counting stars
Visual: all vehicles parked in a row, stars appearing. Tap: each tap adds a star, up to five.
- "Let's count the stars. One, two, three, four, five." / "Jom kira bintang. Satu, dua, tiga, empat, lima."

## Scene 7: Lullaby (tap-free)
Visual: screen dims slowly, vehicles' lights go out, moon glows. Soft music box melody.
Lullaby (EN), sung slowly:
  Little Jeep, close your eyes,
  Moon is shining in the skies.
  Bus and lorry, train and car,
  Sleeping under every star.
Lullaby (BM):
  Jip kecil, tutup mata,
  Bulan bersinar di angkasa.
  Bas dan lori, kereta api,
  Tidur lena hingga pagi.

## Scene 8: Goodnight
Visual: fades to a dark starry screen, then the screen turns itself off after the music ends.
- "Goodnight, Little Jeep. Goodnight, little one." / "Selamat malam, Jip Kecil. Selamat malam, sayang."

Build notes: no sudden sounds, volume fades out at the end, a "play again" button only appears after a parent taps the corner, and the whole story lasts about 4 minutes.
