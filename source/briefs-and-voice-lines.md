# Little Racers: Game Briefs and Voice Lines (EN / BM)
Rules for every game: big tap targets, no reading needed, no fail screens, voice prompts, no ads, English/BM toggle. Wrong answers get a gentle wobble plus a hint, never a buzzer.

## Age 2
### 1. Beep Beep!
Goal: cause and effect. Screen: a row of 4 vehicles (car, bus, motorbike, fire engine). Tap makes a sound, a wiggle and a spoken name.
- [01] Intro: "Tap a car!" / "Tekan kereta!"
- [02] Car: "Pon pon! Car!" / "Pon pon! Kereta!"
- [03] Bus: "Bus! Vroom!" / "Bas! Vroom!"
- [04] Motorbike: "Motorbike! Ngeeng!" / "Motosikal! Ngeeng!"
- [05] Fire engine: "Nee-naw! Fire engine!" / "Nee-naw! Bomba!"

### 2. Car Wash
Goal: fine motor and sensory play. Steps: rub mud off, tap soap, tap water, car sparkles and drives off.
- [06] "Oh no, it's muddy! Rub, rub, rub!" / "Alamak, kotornya! Gosok, gosok, gosok!"
- [07] "Bubbles!" / "Buih sabun!"
- [08] "Splash!" / "Simbah!"
- [09] "So shiny! Bye-bye!" / "Berkilat! Bai-bai!"

### 3. Peekaboo Garage
Goal: object permanence and vocabulary. 3 garage doors; tap one, a vehicle pops out.
- [10] "Who's inside?" / "Siapa di dalam?"
- [11] "Peekaboo! It's a lorry!" / "Ciluk ba! Lori!"
- [12] "Peekaboo! It's a taxi!" / "Ciluk ba! Teksi!"

## Age 3
### 4. Colour Parking
Goal: colour matching. Drag red, yellow, blue, green cars to matching spots.
- [13] "Park the red car!" / "Parkir kereta merah!"
- [14] Correct: "Yes! Red goes here!" / "Betul! Merah di sini!"
- [15] Hint: "Find the yellow spot." / "Cari tempat kuning."

### 5. Traffic Light
Goal: road safety, impulse control. Car drives only on green.
- [16] "Green means go!" / "Hijau, jalan!"
- [17] "Red means stop!" / "Merah, berhenti!"
- [18] "Yellow, slow down." / "Kuning, perlahan."
- [19] Reward: "Good driver!" / "Pemandu yang bagus!"

### 6. Build My Car
Goal: part-whole thinking. Snap on body, wheels, roof, then GO.
- [20] "Let's build a car!" / "Jom bina kereta!"
- [21] "Wheels on! One, two, three, four!" / "Pasang tayar! Satu, dua, tiga, empat!"
- [22] "Ready? Go!" / "Sedia? Jalan!"

## Age 4
### 7. Count the Cars (Toll Plaza)
Goal: counting 1 to 10. Cars queue at a toll; tap the number.
- [23] "How many cars at the toll?" / "Berapa kereta di tol?"
- [24] Correct: "Five cars! Hooray!" / "Lima kereta! Hore!"
- [25] Hint: "Let's count together." / "Jom kira sama-sama."

### 8. Road Maze
Goal: path planning. Trace a road to home, school or the pasar malam. No walls to crash into.
- [26] "Help the car get to the pasar malam!" / "Bantu kereta ke pasar malam!"
- [27] "You made it!" / "Dah sampai!"

### 9. Shape Garage
Goal: shapes. Each car carries a shape; drive it into the matching tunnel.
- [28] "This car has a circle." / "Kereta ini ada bulatan."
- Shapes: circle/bulatan, square/segi empat sama, triangle/segi tiga.
- [29] "Perfect fit!" / "Muat-muat!"

## Age 5
### 10. Letter Road
Goal: phonics. Steer to collect letters that spell CAR, BUS, VAN (BM mode: BAS, VAN, LORI).
- [30] "Collect C, A, R. Car!" / "Kutip B, A, S. Bas!"
- [31] "B says buh, B for Bas!" / "B, bunyinya beh, B untuk Bas!"
- [32] "You spelled it!" / "Pandainya mengeja!"

### 11. Big Little Race
Goal: ordering by size. Sort smallest to biggest, then they race.
- [33] "Smallest first!" / "Yang paling kecil dulu!"
- [34] "Small, bigger, biggest!" / "Kecil, lebih besar, paling besar!"
- [35] "Ready, set, go!" / "Sedia, mula, jalan!"

### 12. Helper Trucks
Goal: community helpers. Match the vehicle to the scene.
- [36] "Fire! Get out, stay out, and tell a grown-up to call bomba, 999!" / "Kebakaran! Keluar cepat, jangan masuk semula, dan minta orang dewasa telefon bomba, 999!"
- [37] "Ouch, a hurt knee! Tell a grown-up, and if it's serious, call 999 for the ambulance." / "Aduh, lutut luka! Beritahu orang dewasa, dan jika teruk, telefon 999 untuk ambulans."
- [38] "The car stopped working. Stay inside with your seatbelt on while Tow Truck helps!" / "Kereta rosak. Duduk dalam kereta, pakai tali pinggang, Trak Tunda datang membantu!"
- [39] "Everyone is safe. Thank you, helpers!" / "Semua selamat. Terima kasih, wira kita!"

## Bonus: Balik Kampung Road Trip (age 5)
Goal: memory and sequencing. Show 3 landmarks, then the child drives the route in order: petrol station, durian stall, Atuk's house.
- [40] "Remember the way to Atuk's house!" / "Ingat jalan ke rumah Atuk!"
- [41] "First, the petrol station." / "Mula-mula, stesen minyak."
- [42] "Then, the durian stall!" / "Lepas tu, gerai durian!"
- [43] "We're home! Atuk is happy!" / "Dah sampai kampung! Atuk gembira!"
